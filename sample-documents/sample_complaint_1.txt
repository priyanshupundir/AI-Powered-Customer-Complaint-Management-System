COMPLAINT DETAILS
================================================================================

Nature of Complaint: Discolored Capsules
Description: 
We received a shipment of Amoxicylin Capsules 500 mg, Batch AMX-2024-0892, 
on September 5, 2024. Upon inspection, we noticed that approximately 48 capsules 
from the affected lot show visible discoloration. The capsules appear to have 
a yellowish tint instead of the expected white color.

The affected quantity is 48 capsules from a shipment of 1000 capsules.
No adverse patient reactions have been reported to date.

Additional Observations:
- Discoloration is visible on both capsule halves
- No unusual odor detected
- Capsules appear to be intact (no cracks or leaks)
- Storage conditions were maintained as per manufacturer recommendations
