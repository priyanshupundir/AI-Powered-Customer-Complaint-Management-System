NATURE OF COMPLAINT
================================================================================

Primary Issue: Tablet Hardness/Friability Problem
Secondary Issue: Packaging Integrity Concern

Detailed Description:
We received Ciprofloxacin Tablets 500 mg, Batch CHG260712A, on September 8, 2024.
During routine quality inspection, our pharmacy team identified the following issues:

1. Tablet Hardness: Several tablets showed excessive hardness, making them
   difficult to crush for pediatric dose preparation
2. Friability: Friability testing showed 1.2% (specification: ≤0.8%)
3. Packaging: 3 bottles from the shipment had compromised seals
4. Appearance: Slight color variation observed in approximately 10% of tablets

Affected Quantity: 200 tablets from a 500-tablet shipment
Number of Affected Bottles: 5 out of 10 bottles received

Clinical Impact:
- Delayed medication administration due to crushing difficulties
- Potential dose accuracy concerns for pediatric patients
- Medication wastage during preparation
